Zakiy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

EnglishO'zbekcha
Summary. Zakiy is an educational app for children up to grade 6. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not ask children for their name, email, photos, or location. Learning progress is stored on the device. We use Google Firebase to understand app usage and fix crashes, and Google Play to process subscriptions.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Zakiy mobile application ("the App"), published on Google Play by Dimapps Group ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at dimapps.group@gmail.com.

2. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and their parents. We follow the Google Play Families Policy and aim to comply with children's privacy laws, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • We do not ask children to enter personal information such as name, email address, phone number, photos, or address.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not collect the Android Advertising ID.
  • The App contains no advertising and no personalized advertising.
  • The App has no chat, social features, or public profiles.
  • Purchases can only be made through Google Play, which applies its own parental controls.

3. Information we collect

DataWhyWhere it is processed
Learning progress (completed puzzles, scores, settings)To let the child continue where they stoppedStored only on the device. We do not receive it.
App usage events (e.g. screen opened, lesson completed), app version, device model, OS version, an app instance IDTo understand which features are used and to improve lessonsGoogle Firebase Analytics

This data does not identify the child by name. We configure Firebase Analytics with Advertising ID collection disabled and ad personalization signals disabled.

Payments. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store card numbers or other payment details. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the App and save learning progress on the device.
  • To fix crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the App is used so we can improve learning content.
  • To verify and manage subscriptions.

We do not use data for advertising, profiling, or behavioral targeting.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent data. We share data only with these service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Google LLC — Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Firebase privacy information).
  • Google LLC — Google Play Billing for subscriptions (Google Privacy Policy).

We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention

  • Learning progress stays on the device until the App is deleted or its data is cleared.
  • Firebase Analytics event data is kept for the shortest retention period available in Firebase settings.
  • Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

7. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission to send diagnostics and to check subscription status. The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

8. Security

Data sent from the App to our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

9. Parents' rights and choices

  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings removes all learning progress stored on the device.
  • Parents may contact us at dimapps.group@gmail.com to ask what data relates to their child's device or to request its deletion. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Future accounts

We plan to add optional parent accounts (sign-in with Google or email) and cloud sync of progress. These features are not available yet. Before we launch them, we will update this Privacy Policy, describe exactly what data is collected, and request verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than the user's country, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Any change will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
